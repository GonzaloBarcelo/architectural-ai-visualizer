## Architectural Rendering Prompt — Warm Mediterranean Luxury Minimalism

Transform the provided architectural image into a **high-end, photorealistic interior visualization**, following a sophisticated **Warm Mediterranean Luxury Minimalism** aesthetic.

### 1. Architectural Fidelity
- Strictly preserve the original room geometry, proportions, ceiling heights, wall positions, windows, doors, and architectural openings.
- Maintain the original camera position, perspective, and spatial composition.
- Respect the existing furniture layout and functional distribution while upgrading materials, finishes, and furnishings.
- Do not invent architectural features or alter the structural layout.

### 2. Design Style
Create an atmosphere of **understated luxury, timeless elegance, and contemporary Mediterranean sophistication**, combining Scandinavian minimalism with warm natural materials.

The interior should feel serene, refined, spacious, and exceptionally comfortable.

Avoid excessive decoration, visual clutter, or ostentatious luxury.

### 3. Materials & Color Palette
Use a harmonious palette of:
- Warm ivory, cream, sand, and soft beige.
- Natural light oak and subtly textured wood.
- Travertine, limestone, and honed natural stone.
- Muted olive green and sage accents.
- Natural linen, bouclé, cotton, and woven textiles.
- Subtle brushed brass details.

Favor matte surfaces, tactile finishes, organic textures, and natural material variation.

### 4. Furniture & Decoration
- Contemporary furniture with clean lines, balanced proportions, and soft silhouettes.
- Low-profile upholstered sofas and beds in warm neutral fabrics.
- Custom minimalist cabinetry with integrated handles.
- Sculptural stone or natural wood coffee tables.
- Large textured neutral rugs.
- Carefully curated ceramics, abstract artwork, and understated accessories.
- Mediterranean greenery, including olive trees and subtle botanical arrangements.

Every element should communicate quality, comfort, and restraint.

### 5. Lighting & Atmosphere
Create realistic, cinematic architectural lighting:
- Soft, warm natural daylight entering through existing windows.
- Gentle sunlight creating organic shadows across walls and floors.
- Warm indirect architectural lighting, approximately 2700–3000K.
- Subtle integrated LED lighting beneath cabinetry or within existing architectural details.
- Elegant table lamps with soft diffused illumination.
- Physically accurate global illumination, reflections, ambient occlusion, and natural shadow transitions.

The atmosphere should evoke a peaceful Mediterranean residence during the late afternoon.

### 6. Photorealistic Rendering
Produce a premium architectural visualization comparable to an editorial feature in Architectural Digest.

Rendering characteristics:
- Ultra-photorealistic materials and textures.
- Physically based rendering (PBR).
- Accurate light transport and global illumination.
- Realistic material roughness and subtle imperfections.
- Balanced exposure with natural white balance.
- Soft cinematic contrast.
- High dynamic range.
- Professional architectural photography composition.
- Sharp architectural details without artificial oversharpening.
- High-resolution output.

### 7. Visual Consistency
Maintain exactly the same design language across all rooms in the project.

The bedroom, living room, kitchen, dining area, and bathrooms must belong to the same architectural residence, sharing consistent materials, colors, lighting philosophy, and furniture quality.

### 8. Strict Constraints
**Do not modify the original architectural geometry, camera perspective, room dimensions, or functional arrangement.**

Do not introduce unnecessary luxury elements such as excessive marble, glossy gold, ornate moldings, or extravagant furniture.

Prioritize **natural elegance, architectural authenticity, visual harmony, and understated sophistication**.

### Final Artistic Direction
A serene, sunlit Mediterranean luxury residence with warm neutral tones, organic natural materials, minimalist contemporary furniture, refined architectural detailing, and a timeless, understated atmosphere.

**The final result must look like a real photograph of an exceptionally designed luxury home, not a computer-generated model.**
